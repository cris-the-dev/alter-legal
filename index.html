How do I cancel a subscription?

iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions → Alter.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open Alter → Settings → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.

Does Alter add a watermark?

No. Your saved photos are yours, free or Pro.
